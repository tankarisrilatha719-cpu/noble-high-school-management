About Noble High School

Noble High School has served families in and around Kandukur, Rangareddy District, since 1995. We teach children from Nursery, LKG and UKG through Class 10, under the leadership of our principal, Bairu Kondal Reddy. Parents can apply online, reach the office by phone or WhatsApp, and follow their child's progress through the student portal.

Admin Portal › Backup & Restore

Backup & Restore

What this does: Backup downloads every piece of information stored on this website — going back to the very first academic year, not just the current one. Every student you ever added, every fee payment you ever recorded, every complaint, every notification, every uploaded photo — all of it goes into a single file. If you ever lose data, just upload that file back here and your website returns to exactly the state it was in when you took the backup.

Download Full Backup

Saves everything — from the first academic year you started using this website up to today — into one downloadable file. Take a backup before any major change (year update, school out, bulk edits, etc.).

Restore from Backup

️ Warning: This will replace ALL current data on the website with the contents of your backup file. Everything currently here will be lost — take a fresh backup first if you're not sure.

Fee Payment Receipts

Every time a parent pays the fee, a receipt is automatically saved here. You can download a single receipt as PDF, or export all payment history as Excel for record-keeping.
